Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data SuiteClose collects through suiteclose.co.uk, why, and what your rights are under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

SuiteClose is a NetSuite data engineering service run by Abhisekh Kumar, based in the United Kingdom. SuiteClose is the data controller for the personal data described below. You can contact us about this notice or your data through the contact form.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company (optional) and your message.
  • Website assistant (chat): the messages you type. If you ask to be contacted and agree, the assistant also passes on your name, email, company and a short summary of your enquiry.
  • Free AI Readiness Audit: your answers and score. If you choose to enter your email address, we receive it together with your answers so we can send you your results.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site. The website assistant keeps your current conversation in your browser's session storage so it survives page changes; it is cleared when you close the tab.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use this data only to reply to your enquiry, send you the results you asked for, and discuss whether SuiteClose can help. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and, where you request something from us, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We do not sell your data or add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it for us

  • Formspree receives contact form and audit submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model behind the website assistant. Your chat messages are sent to Anthropic to generate replies; Anthropic does not use them to train its models.
  • Netlify hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on this site, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and audit submissions for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless we enter into a contract, in which case we keep records for as long as needed for that contract and our legal obligations.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, contact us through the contact form. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
